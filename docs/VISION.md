# ZumZum Vision

## Problem

Phone-number-first messengers make a private telecom identifier double as a long-lived social address. If that address is exposed in a video, group, screenshot or leak, the user often has limited ways to rotate the public entry point without disrupting the account itself.

ZumZum should separate the account from the public identifiers used to reach it.

## Product direction

ZumZum should feel fast and simple enough for everyday chat while supporting richer paths over time: direct messages, groups, communities/channels, media, temporary contact links, business inboxes and native RUNA assistance.

It should not require a phone number to be the public identity of the account. Recovery and trust mechanisms may use verified email, passkeys, device trust and optional stronger identity verification.

## Channel strategy

ZumZum does not need to replace WhatsApp on day one. Especially in Brazil, WhatsApp can remain an important external channel while ZumZum grows its own network and value.

For businesses, the long-term value can be a unified communication layer where ZumZum, web chat and approved external channels are handled through the same RUNA/business workflow.

## Product principle

Communication should remain easy and broadly accessible. Paid plans should sell convenience, personalization, organization, automation and scale - not basic trust or the right to communicate.
