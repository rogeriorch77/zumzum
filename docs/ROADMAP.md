# Product Roadmap - Draft

This is a sequencing draft, not a delivery promise.

## Phase 0 - Product contracts

- identity model: Internal Account ID, ZumID, handle, temporary links;
- privacy/discovery rules;
- account recovery/passkeys;
- personal vs business contract;
- abuse/threat model;
- message/data model.

## Phase 1 - Lightweight messenger MVP

- account creation;
- direct messages;
- contact requests;
- ZumID + QR;
- basic handles;
- block/report;
- device/session management;
- minimal media support.

## Phase 2 - Groups and migration

- groups;
- controlled invite links;
- import pipeline for supported messenger exports;
- native/imported provenance in history.

## Phase 3 - Business

- active-business validation;
- business profiles;
- shared/team inbox;
- catalog/business metadata;
- roles/audit;
- first analytics.

## Phase 4 - RUNA

- personal explicit-assist actions;
- RUNA Sales for businesses;
- translation/summarization/drafting;
- approved business automation.

## Later exploration

- communities/channels;
- richer creator features;
- additional countries/business registries;
- deeper cross-channel integrations;
- local/offline capabilities where practical.
