# Business Accounts

## Business means formally registered business

A ZumZum Business account should require an active CNPJ in Brazil (or the equivalent formal business identifier in other supported countries). It must not be a self-selected label that any personal account can enable.

The validation provider/API is still to be selected. The system should validate the active business registration and, where appropriate, the responsible person.

## Informal sellers

People who are starting a business without formal registration can continue using a personal account. They may:

- sell products or services;
- use a display/business fantasy name;
- purchase a custom handle such as `@docesdamaria`;
- receive normal messages and use personal-account commerce-friendly features.

They must not be presented as a formally verified business until the business is legally registered and validated.

## Why a business should want Business

Business status must unlock practical operational value, not cosmetic badges. Candidate capabilities:

- verified company identity;
- business profile and branded handle included or reserved;
- multiple authorized attendants/admins;
- shared/team inbox;
- catalog and service information;
- office hours and automated availability rules;
- RUNA Sales integration;
- customer organization and tags;
- analytics and service-quality metrics;
- automation and approved workflows;
- API/webhooks/integrations;
- migration/import tooling for existing customer history;
- role-based access and audit trail.

The business should feel a real operational reason to formalize and upgrade.

## Migration from personal to business

A seller who formalizes later should be able to link/migrate the commercial identity without losing the personal history they are allowed to retain. Personal conversations and business operations should become better separated, not merged into one chaotic inbox.

## Verification is not for sale

A business subscription can pay for business tools and scale. It cannot buy a verification mark if the legal identity has not been validated.
