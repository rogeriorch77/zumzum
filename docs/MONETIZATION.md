# Monetization

## Principle

**Free to communicate. Paid to personalize, organize, automate and operate at scale.**

ZumZum should avoid monetization that makes basic safety or authenticity a luxury.

## Personal paid value

Possible paid features:

- premium/custom handles;
- additional aliases;
- more appearance/profile customization;
- expanded storage or media retention options;
- additional temporary-link controls;
- convenience features that do not create a fake trust signal.

ZumID rotation can have reasonable free limits. Paid plans may increase convenience, but no plan should allow unlimited identity rotation that enables abuse.

## Business paid value

Business plans can charge for capabilities with clear operational ROI:

- multiple agents/admins;
- shared inbox;
- automation;
- RUNA Sales capacity;
- analytics;
- catalog/business tools;
- API/webhooks;
- integrations;
- storage/history policies;
- higher approved messaging/service limits.

## What money cannot buy

- identity verification;
- business-verification status without a valid registered business;
- safety/reputation overrides;
- unlimited identifier rotation for abuse evasion.
