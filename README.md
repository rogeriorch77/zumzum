# ZumZum

Status: concept / product planning.

ZumZum is a lightweight communication platform inspired by the convenience of modern messengers while aiming to reduce dependence on phone-number identity and give users stronger control over how they can be discovered and contacted.

The goal is not to copy WhatsApp, Telegram, WeChat or Snap feature-for-feature. The goal is to combine useful communication patterns in a simpler product with strong privacy controls, flexible identity and native AI assistance through RUNA.

## Product pillars

- no public phone-number dependency;
- simple, lightweight chat experience;
- rotatable public contact identity;
- optional human-readable handles;
- personal and verified business identities kept distinct;
- trust cannot be purchased;
- import/migration paths from other messengers where technically and legally possible;
- RUNA as an integrated assistant, not a separate pasted-on chatbot;
- free communication, paid convenience/organization/scale.
